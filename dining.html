Fine Dining

Il Faro.

Our Michelin-recognized dining room seats just twenty-eight, on a terrace suspended above the water. The tasting menu changes with the tide and the grove — a recent evening closed with a slow-braised lamb shoulder finished tableside over citrus embers.
