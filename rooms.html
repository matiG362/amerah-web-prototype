Rooms & Suites

Thirty-four rooms.
Not one alike.

Dressed in local stone, hand-loomed linen, and the particular light of this coastline — each room carries a name, a view, and a character of its own.

Our Philosophy

We keep no two rooms identical by design. Rates begin from €480 per night, inclusive of breakfast on the terrace — but we'd rather help you choose the right room than quote you a price. Every stay is arranged personally by our reservations team.
