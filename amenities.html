Pools & Leisure

The infinity pool.

Carved into the cliff face in 1971, the pool holds no visible edge between water and horizon. A second, quieter pool sits within the citrus grove for those who prefer shade and stillness to open sky.

Open Year-Round

Wellness & Spa

Terra Spa.

Set within the original stone cellars of the estate, Terra Spa draws on the citrus, olive, and sea salt of this coastline for every treatment. Our signature ritual — the Bergamot Body Journey — begins with a dry-brush exfoliation using groves-grown citrus oil, followed by a warm stone massage.

Two treatment rooms, a sauna carved from local stone, and a relaxation terrace overlooking the grove complete the space.
