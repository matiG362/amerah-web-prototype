The Setting

Amerah Palace stands in Jigjiga, the dynamic capital of Ethiopia's Somali Region. Situated against the backdrop of broad plateaus and the Karamara hills, the city is a vibrant crossroads of heritage, commerce, and warm hospitality.

Nearby

Experiences worth planning.

Jigjiga Central Market

A bustling market rich with colorful textiles, aromatic frankincense, spices, and artisanal leather goods.

Karamara Mountain Range

A historic mountain pass offering expansive views across the plains, ideal for guided morning drives.

Cultural Tea Gatherings

Experience an authentic tea ceremony featuring local herbs and stories curated by our cultural host.

Artisan Weaving Centers

Watch traditional artisans craft handwoven baskets, textiles, and decorative woodwork.

Plateau Walking Trails

Gentle, guided walking paths across the surrounding highlands with views of the entire valley.

Regional Heritage Landmarks

Discover historical monuments and cultural institutions documenting the story of the Somali Region.
