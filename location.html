The Setting

Amerah Palace sits on a shoulder of cliff between the fishing villages of Portovino and Sant'Elmo, where terraced vineyards meet the sea in a single uninterrupted slope. This stretch of coast has drawn travelers since the nineteenth century — not for what's been built here, but for what hasn't.

Nearby

Days worth staying for.

Portovino

A fifteenth-century fishing village, six minutes by car — best reached by boat at sunrise for the market.

Terrazze Alte Vineyard

Four generations of a single family, still hand-harvesting the steepest terraces on this coast.

Cala Nascosta

A cove accessible only by boat or a forty-minute cliff path — arranged as a private excursion for guests.

Sant'Elmo Market

A Saturday-morning market for anchovies, focaccia, and the region's small-batch olive oil.

The Cliff Path

A well-worn walking trail connecting three villages, with a view of the Villa from nearly every turn.

San Rocco Chapel

An eleventh-century chapel, open at dusk, with what many guests call the finest sunset view on the coast.

When to Visit

A coast for every season.

Late May and June bring citrus blossom and warm, uncrowded days. July and August carry the full heat of the Ligurian summer and the liveliest village evenings. September into October offers our guests' favorite window — warm water, golden light, and the start of the olive harvest.
