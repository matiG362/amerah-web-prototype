Our Story

A sanctuary built
for Jigjiga.

Where classical architectural proportions meet the generous spirit and quiet dignity of the Somali Region.

The Vision

Hospitality rooted in place.

Amerah Palace was established with a singular focus: to offer Jigjiga an accommodation where international standards of comfort exist in harmony with authentic regional character.

Standing seven floors above the city, the hotel was designed to create an oasis of stillness. Outside, Jigjiga moves with vibrant trade, regional commerce, and highland energy; inside our gates, the atmosphere slows to a gentle cadence defined by cool stone, shaded courtyards, and attentive human care.
